# pet-tank.html · 電子雞飼養箱

Scope: a playable web virtual pet (電子雞) on the owner's site, rules simplified from Tamagotchi Paradise as documented in `tamagotchi.html` (field → kid, food cell → young, care misses during the young stage → adult). Visitor mode: **Experience** (the visitor is inside the game). Phone first, desktop too. Owner's answers (2026-10-20): real time (life goes on while the page is closed, caught up on reopen); simplified Paradise rules (fields, field cells, food cells, tornado bands; feeding, play, poop, sleep, illness; no planet levels, space travel or marriage); original characters, 86 in all (egg, baby, 6 kids, 18 youngs, 54 adults in three tornado bands 0–1 / 2–5 / 6+, 6 hidden for perfect care). No Bandai names, sprites or logos. Progress lives in this browser only (localStorage).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The pet lives in a real glass tank on a pet-shop shelf; the six Paradise fields are six kinds of tank (sand terrarium, aquarium, tall perch cage, bamboo rainforest tank, warm-lamp tropical tank, frosted freezer case), and care is done to the tank: food drops through the lid, a knuckle taps the glass to play, a scoop clears poop, a spray bottle grooms, a switch turns the lamp off. Refuses the category default: an egg-shaped device with an LCD and three buttons, and the cartoon-room app with stat bars.

OWN-WORLD: a dim shop wall (near-black teal) lit by the tank's own fluorescent lamp; black plastic rim and lid, faint green-edged glass with reflections; habitat substrate in natural materials per tank. Fluorescent-orange price sticker (name, stage, age) and a yellow index card taped on with clear tape, written in pencil handwriting (Iansui) with 正-tally marks for tornadoes, rice balls and suns, boxes ticked for hunger and mood. UI in Chiron GoRound TC. Creatures built from a few flat colour planes, no outlines. Field colours only as the tank's label band.

STORY: pick a tank for the egg → it hatches → keep it fed, played with, clean, asleep on time → watch the field cells and food cells fill under the magnifier → it grows into one of 86 → it joins the tank's past residents on the shelf and a new egg starts; the binder on the wall fills up.
